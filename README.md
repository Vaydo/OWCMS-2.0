# OWCMS — Oil Well Data Management & Analytics Platform

**Developed by VINX Team (Baku, 2026)**

OWCMS is a unified digital environment that brings every technical and operational record of an oil well together — turning scattered files into live analytics, automated reporting, and data-driven decisions.

## 🔍 The Problem
Operating and repairing oil wells generates massive volumes of daily technical and production data. Currently, most of this data is managed in disconnected Excel spreadsheets and file-based tools, leading to:
* **Scattered Storage:** Files are split across departments with no unified connection.
* **Human Factor:** Manual entry causes errors, duplication, and data inconsistency.
* **No Complete History:** No single view of a well from construction to its latest workover.
* **Delayed Analytics:** Assembling reports manually takes hours, meaning decisions follow the facts instead of leading them.

## 💡 The Solution: Digital "Shakhmatka" & Unified Platform
OWCMS keeps the familiar oilfield **"Shakhmatka"** grid logic intact while transforming it into an interactive digital platform. Every well's lifecycle is stored in a structured, connected chain:
`Well Passport` ➔ `Daily Data` ➔ `Monthly Shakhmatka` ➔ `Annual Dynamics` ➔ `Workover & Repairs` ➔ `Equipment & Failures` ➔ `Production & Loss Impact`

### Key Modules (One System, Eight Views)
1. **Well Construction & Casings:** Guide, conductor, technical/production casings, cement, and filter zones.
2. **Shakhmatka & Daily Data:** Seamless daily data entry with automated monthly and annual rollups.
3. **Technical Regime:** Assigned regime parameters and full historical tracking of changes.
4. **Production & Loss Analytics:** Real-time comparison of actual production against technical regimes to pinpoint loss sources.
5. **Repair & Workover (KRS/PRS):** Downtime tracking, effective time, and brigade-level records.
6. **Equipment & Failures:** Installed pumps, failure root causes, and replacement history.
7. **Field-Office Communication:** Built-in chat, photo/video incident logging, notifications, and video conferencing.
8. **Automated Reporting:** One-click export of daily/monthly reports to Excel and PDF.
